# Lumen 0.3.0 beta for Android: test build

The first Lumen for Android phones, in plain words. **This is a test build**: please try it and say what breaks. Windows stays at 0.2.0 for now.

## What works on Android

- **Your music on the phone:** Lumen reads the **Music** folder (and any other folder you add in *Settings → Add folder*). FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, WavPack, MP3, AAC, OGG and Opus; embedded covers, `folder.jpg` / `cover.jpg` and `.lrc` lyrics.
- **New music shows up** each time you come back to Lumen, or when you pull down on a list.
- **Playback:** play, pause, next, previous, the queue, gapless, shuffle and repeat.
- **Music keeps playing in the background** and with the screen locked. The notification and the lock screen show the song with Previous, Play/Pause and Next. Headphone and Bluetooth buttons work too.
- **Calls and other apps:** a call or another player pauses Lumen; after a short interruption it plays on.
- **Unplugging your headphones** pauses the music.
- **The full Sound page**, the same as on Windows:
  - the graphic and advanced EQ with all 17 presets;
  - headphone correction (AutoEQ);
  - crossfeed, ReplayGain, levelling and night mode;
  - **Best quality on this device**: soxr at its best setting, 32-bit float, a gentle limiter.

  The **signal path** shows honestly what happens: the file, the EQ and the other steps, what Lumen sends, Android's mixer, and where the sound goes (phone speaker, wired, USB or Bluetooth).
- **Lumen's own sound engine** (mpv + FFmpeg with every filter and soxr), built in public at [lumen-audio-engine](https://github.com/capsuleutkarsh-design/lumen-audio-engine).
- **A phone layout:**
  - the tabs at the bottom (Home, Albums, Artists, Songs, Browse, More), with a mini player above them;
  - swipe the mini player up for **Now Playing** with the spinning CD or vinyl;
  - tap to play, long-press for the menu, swipe a song left in the queue to remove it;
  - landscape, and the dark and light themes.
- **The same covers** as on Windows: the art packs and the painted styles, smart playlists, statistics and lyrics.

Windows-only things are hidden on the phone: exclusive mode, the output picker, the tray, the mini window and Snap Layouts.

## How to install it on your OnePlus 6

1. On the phone, open this release page and download **`Lumen-0.3.0-beta-android-arm64-v8a.apk`**. (The other two files are for old 32-bit phones and for emulators; you don't need them.)
2. Open the downloaded file, from the browser's download message or from the **Files** app.
3. Android asks to **allow installs from this app** (the browser or the file manager). Allow it once, go back, and tap **Install**.
4. Open **Lumen**.

## The first start

Lumen shows a screen that says it needs to read your music. Tap **Allow access**, then **Allow** in Android's question ("Allow Lumen to access photos and media"). Lumen only reads your files; it never changes them. Your **Music** folder is then read at once.

## Tip: the best Bluetooth sound (LDAC)

Apps can't choose the Bluetooth codec on Android 11, but you can. With LDAC headphones connected:

1. Turn on **Developer options** once: *Settings → About phone*, tap **Build number** seven times.
2. Open *Settings → System → Developer options*:
   - **Bluetooth audio codec**: LDAC;
   - **Bluetooth audio sample rate**: 96 kHz;
   - **Bluetooth audio bits per sample**: 24-bit;
   - **Bluetooth audio LDAC codec: Playback quality**: *Optimized for audio quality* (990 kbps).

Even then Bluetooth is lossy; a wired or USB headphone is the clean way. Lumen's Sound page shows this tip when Bluetooth headphones are playing.

## Known limits

- Files copied by a computer or a file manager appear at once. Files put on the phone some other way (for example by `adb`) only appear after the phone has indexed them.
- Folders on an SD card or USB stick can't be added yet; the phone's own storage works.
- On Android 13 and newer, Lumen asks only for audio, so `folder.jpg` covers next to the songs may not show there (embedded covers do). Your OnePlus 6 (Android 11) shows them.
- No output picker, exclusive mode or bit-perfect USB on Android yet. Android always mixes the sound.
- Calls pausing the music could not be tried on the emulator; please try a call on your phone.
- Saving files (M3U8 export, EQ preset export) is not on Android yet.

## This is a TEST build

It is signed with a **temporary key** (the debug key of the build computer). Android only installs an update over an app with the same key. So when the first properly signed Lumen comes, **uninstall this test build first**, then install the new one. Your library is read again from your music; your play counts, playlists and settings in this test build go with the uninstall.
