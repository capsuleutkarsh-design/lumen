# Gleo 0.4.1 beta

A small update for bedtime listening, on Windows and Android.

## New

- **Custom sleep timer.** Besides 15, 30, 45 and 60 minutes, *Custom…* lets you pick any length from 1 minute to 4 hours. The slider moves in 5-minute steps, and − and + change it by one minute. It shows when the music will pause ("Pauses at 1:42 AM"), and Gleo remembers the length you picked last.
- **The time left shows** wherever the timer shows: "Pauses at 1:42 AM · 12 min left" in the player bar's menu, on the phone's Now Playing, and on the lock-screen view.
- **Bedtime.** One tap in the sleep timer menu starts a timer with your last custom length (45 minutes the first time). Over its last 5 minutes the volume fades down slowly, then the music pauses. While Bedtime is on, the moon has a small star.
- **Start Bedtime automatically** (Settings → Bedtime; off unless you turn it on). Pick a time, for example 11:00 PM. When music plays after that time, Bedtime starts by itself once a night and says "Bedtime on: pauses at … · Cancel". If you cancel it, it stays off until the next night. It waits with a single timer, so it costs no battery.

## Battery (Android)

- **Fewer wake-ups while the screen is off.** The audio engine used to tell the app the playing position about 20 times a second, even with the screen off. Now, when Gleo is out of sight, it checks the position twice a second instead, and not at all while paused. On the test phone (Android 11 emulator, screen off, a 24-bit FLAC playing), Gleo's main thread now wakes about 10 times a second instead of 24, and Gleo uses about 0.8 % of one processor core. When the music is paused, Gleo now uses almost nothing (0.02 % of a core).
- **The lock-screen view no longer keeps the screen on.** It used to stay lit while music played. Now the screen stays on for one minute after you wake the phone or touch the view, then turns off as usual while the music keeps playing.
- **Checked, and fine:** when music pauses (by you or by the sleep timer), Gleo lets go of its wake lock and its foreground service, so the phone can sleep. Nothing keeps running after the sleep timer pauses. Nothing draws while the screen is off.
- **Good to know:** the sleep timer stops the music once. If you press Play again later, the music plays on until you stop it. Bedtime's automatic start runs only once a night.
