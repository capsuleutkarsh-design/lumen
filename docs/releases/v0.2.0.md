# Lumen 0.2.0 beta

Everything since 0.1.1, in plain words. Your library from 0.1.x upgrades by itself on the first start; your music files are only ever read.

## New

### Sound
A new **Sound** page (the sliders button in the rail, just above Settings).
- **Signal path** at the top shows what really happens to the music, step by step, for example `FLAC 24/96 → EQ → Limiter → 48 kHz → Speakers`. A **Bit-perfect ✓** badge appears only when Lumen has checked that the device gets exactly the samples in the file. Otherwise it says plainly why not.
- **Equalizer:** a 10-band **Graphic** EQ, or an **Advanced** parametric EQ with up to 10 bands (peak, shelves, low-/high-pass, notch) that you can drag on the curve. Moving a slider changes the sound live, with no gap or click. 17 built-in presets (including *Small speakers* and *Late night*), plus your own: save, rename, delete, export and import.
- **Headphone correction:** paste or open an AutoEQ `ParametricEQ.txt`, an Equalizer APO file or a `GraphicEQ.txt`. Nothing is downloaded.
- **EQ per output device:** your headphones and your speakers each keep their own EQ.
- **Crossfeed** for headphones (Light / Medium / Strong), so music feels less "inside your head".
- **Best quality on this device** (on by default): when the device runs at another rate, Lumen converts with soxr at its best setting, works in 32-bit float, adds fine dither for whole-number devices, lowers the level by exactly the EQ's highest boost and keeps peaks under −0.3 dBFS. Cheap devices get clean sound with no crackle.
- **Upsampling** to 88.2/96, 176.4/192 kHz or the device maximum (in Exclusive mode).
- **ReplayGain** (Track / Album / Auto) with clipping prevention, and optional gentle levelling for files without ReplayGain tags.
- The output device and Exclusive mode moved here from Settings.

### Covers and the spinning disc
- **Cover art for albums without a good cover.** When an album has no cover, or a small or blurry one, Lumen shows art instead. It comes from three collections, all on by default:
  - **Masterpieces:** 100 public-domain paintings (van Gogh, Hokusai, Klimt, Turner and more), credited in the picker.
  - **Illustrated:** 100 storybook illustrations made for Lumen.
  - **Painted:** three styles that Lumen paints itself (starry night, storybook and garden), so the variety never runs out.
  - **Abstract:** Lumen's 11 code-drawn styles, off by default, can be turned on in Settings → Disc & covers.
- **Change cover:** right-click an album (or the big cover in Now Playing) to pick any artwork, or keep the original. Your pick is saved for that album and survives moving your music folder. Music files are never written.
- Each collection is used about equally often, and the drawn art is cached on disk (up to about 300 MB, the least recently used goes first).
- **The spinning disc:** in Now Playing, a disc slides out from behind the cover, printed with the album art, and turns while music plays. It slows to a stop on pause. **CD** (default) or **vinyl**, or off, in Settings.
- **Disc mode** (press **D**): only the cover and the spinning disc, for a second screen. F11 fills the screen; Esc leaves.
- **Mini player:** a small always-on-top window with the cover, the disc and the controls. Open it from ••• in the player bar or from the tray.

### Browse, playlists and statistics
- **Browse** (the compass in the rail): **Genres**, **Years** (by decade), **Composers** (each work with its movements in order; *Play work* plays them all), **Duplicates** (copies side by side, with their format; *Hide from library* hides one without touching the file) and **Statistics**.
- **Statistics:** listening time per day, week or month, and your top songs, albums and artists for 7 days, 30 days, 12 months or all time, plus the size of your library.
- **Smart playlists** that update themselves: rules for genre, year, recently added, play count, never played, favourites, format, sample rate, bit depth and artist. Four are ready-made: Recently added, Most played, Never played and Hi-Res.

### Look and feel
- **Light theme:** Settings → Theme → Dark / Light / Follow Windows. Every text colour passes the AA contrast rule in both.
- **Accent from the album art:** Now Playing's glow and the active seek bar take a colour from the playing song's cover.
- **Tray icon:** play/pause, next, previous, the mini player and quit. *Close to tray* (off by default) keeps the music playing when you close the window.
- **Sleep timer** (the moon button): 15–60 minutes, end of song or end of queue, with a 10-second fade.
- **Queue:** Clear, Save as playlist, Undo after a remove, and *Stop after this song*.
- **Multi-select** everywhere (click, Ctrl+click, Shift+click, Ctrl+A), right-click menus on every card and row, and Play / Shuffle / Play next / Add to queue on every album, artist, folder and playlist.

### Lumen's own audio engine
Lumen now plays through its own build of mpv 0.41 and FFmpeg 8.1, made in public at [github.com/capsuleutkarsh-design/lumen-audio-engine](https://github.com/capsuleutkarsh-design/lumen-audio-engine). It can only open local files (no network at all), it is smaller than before, and it has every filter the Sound page needs.

## Fixed
- **Playback:** gapless hand-overs never play a different song from the one the queue shows, even when you edit the queue at the last moment; Next pressed during a hand-over moves exactly one song; songs that can't be played are skipped with a message instead of hanging; an unplugged output device falls back to the system default; the format badge shows what the device really gets; media keys Play and Pause do exactly that; skips and plays are counted correctly across restarts.
- **Library:** a damaged library file is set aside and Lumen starts fresh instead of failing; renamed or moved music keeps its plays, favourites and playlist places; folders that can't be read no longer remove their songs; better tag reading (DSD, APE, odd MP3/AAC/M4A files, Windows-1252 WAV tags); folder art added later is picked up; album artists and multi-disc albums are grouped better; scanning never blocks the app.
- **Window and start-up:** the window never opens off-screen; if Lumen can't start it says why and offers *Start fresh* (nothing is deleted); only one Lumen runs at a time; a log file (`lumen.log`) for bug reports; Snap Layouts on the maximise button.
- **Layout:** the player bar, album headers and song lists fit small windows and large text; keyboard use (Space, Enter, Tab, arrows) works as expected; scroll position and sort order are remembered.
- **Installer:** the version comes from one place; uninstall asks before deleting your data (No is the default); the portable zip works everywhere; the VC++ runtime is included, so Lumen starts on a fresh Windows.

## Good to know
- Monet's paintings will join the Masterpieces collection in January 2027, when they become public domain.
- The installer is larger (about 40 MB) because the two cover collections are bundled. Nothing is downloaded at any time.
